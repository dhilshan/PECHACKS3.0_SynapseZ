# 💸 FinHealth360 — The Future of Financial + Health Wellness

### 🧩 Domain: FinTech × Healthcare (PEC HACKS 3.0)

---

## 🚀 Overview
**FinHealth360** is an AI-powered platform that bridges the gap between **financial behaviour** and **physical well-being**.  
Our insight: *Financial stress and health problems are deeply connected — yet no tool tracks both together.*

FinHealth360 visualizes how your spending, income patterns, and lifestyle choices impact your overall wellness — and provides **personalized, actionable recommendations** to improve both.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement
Most people manage their **money** and **health** separately.  
- Financial apps show transactions, budgets, or savings.  
- Health apps show steps, calories, or sleep data.  
But **nobody connects the dots** — overspending, stress, and poor lifestyle choices often form a hidden loop that impacts long-term wellness.

---

## 💡 Our Solution
FinHealth360 fuses personal finance data with health metrics to give a **360° Wellness Score** and **insightful correlations**, like:  

> 💰 “Your weekend impulse spending correlates with 35% lower sleep quality.”  
> 🧠 “Frequent food delivery spikes both your monthly spend and your stress markers.”  

It then suggests small but impactful habits to fix these patterns — turning awareness into real improvement.

---

## ⚙️ Key Features
✅ **Unified Dashboard** — Track finances and health on one intuitive screen.  
✅ **AI Correlation Engine** — Detects hidden links between spending patterns and lifestyle choices.  
✅ **Personalized Recommendations** — Smart nudges to improve wellness (e.g., budget reminders, fitness goals).  
✅ **Gamified Insights** — Earn “Wellness Points” for good financial and health habits.  
✅ **Privacy-First Design** — Local processing with anonymized data for security.

---

## 🧠 How It Works
1. **Data Inputs**
   - Financial: Income, expense categories, savings goals (mocked or API-simulated).  
   - Health: Steps, sleep, heart rate, calorie data (via wearable API or manual input).  

2. **Processing Engine**
   - Normalizes both datasets.  
   - Runs statistical and ML-based correlation analysis.  
   - Computes dynamic *FinHealth Index* (0–100).

3. **Visualization**
   - Interactive dashboard (built with React + Flask backend).  
   - Graphs show spending vs. sleep, income stability vs. stress index, etc.  
   - Recommendations update dynamically.

---

## 🧩 Tech Stack
- **Frontend:** React.js / Next.js  
- **Backend:** Python (Flask or FastAPI)  
- **Database:** SQLite / MongoDB  
- **Machine Learning:** scikit-learn, pandas  
- **Visualization:** Chart.js / Plotly  
- **APIs:** Health data (Google Fit / Fitbit), Financial data (Plaid API or mock)  

---

## 🧪 Example Use Case
1. A user connects their bank + wearable data.  
2. FinHealth360 detects that they spend heavily on weekends and sleep less on Sunday nights.  
3. The app recommends a budget cap and an earlier bedtime routine.  
4. Over two weeks, their stress score improves and savings increase — both visualized on the dashboard.
